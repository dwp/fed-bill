Respond to a request for information

Use this service to respond to a request for information (RFI) from an Authorised Officer. Requests are issued under Section 109BZA of the Social Security Administration Act 1992 (as amended by the Social Security Fraud Act 2001).

You can:

  • upload files
  • send a written response
  • submit more than one response if needed

This request is in relation to the provision of information under the Social Security Administration Act 1992 made by an authorised Officer. It does not request, nor authorise the provision of information that would be defined as Communication Data under section 261 of the Investigatory Powers Act 2016.

Before you start

You can find your reference number and access code in the email or letter we sent you.  You do not have to send all the information we have asked for at once.
